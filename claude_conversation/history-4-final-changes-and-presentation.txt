  variant that keeps 24 months but gives older rows less weight.

  Why we tried it

  1. The setting had no justification. TRAIN_START = 2025-10-01 came from the
  first AI session (history-1). The date first appears in an exploratory query
  around line 653 of history-1-setup-and-mvp.full.txt. It was then hard-coded

  gain. That was a real hypothesis, not busywork.

  2025?" needed an answer backed by data, not "the AI picked it".

  it: the window length doesn't matter. This fits the baseline finding. The
