  Verdict: the engineering is strong, but the process signal (which the brief
  calls its main criterion) and the presentation are weaker.

  Strengths
  - No data leakage, and it's proven: the tests rebuild every feature using only
    what was knowable at that moment and check nothing changes. A
    negative-control test, plus a deliberately injected leak in the log, shows
    the tests would catch a real one.
  - Latest data used: the official file stops on 2026-09-07. That was caught,
    and the volunteer file was used so the test period runs to 12 Oct.
  - Careful feature selection: each idea was tested on two separate validation
    windows, with uncertainty that accounts for alerts coming in waves.
  - Failed ideas are documented: weather, sin/cos encoding and the "+5 min"
    count were all tested and rejected with numbers.
  - The product works: the dashboard reuses the training feature code, and the
    trained models are committed, so judges don't need to retrain.

  Main weaknesses
  1. The AI caught most of the mistakes, not you. It corrected its own wrong
     Luhansk claim and tested its own leakage test. Your contributions are
     mostly good questions and feature ideas. At the end of log 2 the AI calls
     the duration model "clearly better" than the baseline on a 0.7-minute MAE
     gain, and nobody challenged it.
  2. The start-model baseline is weak. I checked this with a scratchpad script:

  | Model                                                          | Log loss
  (lower is better) |
  |----------------------------------------------------------------|------------
  ----------------|
  | Your README baseline                                           | 0.525
                 |
  | Logistic regression on one feature (alerts in the last 7 days) | 0.466
                 |
  | LightGBM (yours)                                               | 0.455
                 |
     About 85% of the claimed gain is just "recent activity". The alert rate
     rose from 16% in training to 24.5% in the test weeks, and the year-long
     baseline misses that.

  4. "Now" mode mixes fresh and stale data: the neighbour features still come
     from the daily file while the map shows live state. Live alerts are also

  5. Weibull fits durations better than log-normal on validation, but log-normal

     the brief says "Time Series Analysis".
  7. The optional Telegram data was dropped.
